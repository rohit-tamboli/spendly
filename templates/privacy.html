Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide when creating an account (such as your name and email address) and the expense data you enter while using Spendly, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We also collect basic usage data such as page views to help us improve the service.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the Spendly service — displaying your expense history, generating summaries, and personalising your experience. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. You may request a copy or deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly may use third-party services for infrastructure and analytics (such as hosting providers). These services are bound by their own privacy policies and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes. We do not integrate with any advertising networks.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the contact information provided on our website. We aim to respond to all privacy-related enquiries within 5 business days.
